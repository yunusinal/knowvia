# Office and IT Guidelines

> Synthetic demo document: entirely fictional; not related to any real organization or person.

Laptop Security Basics. Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk (Windows+L). Company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker; never disable it. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours.

Software Request Policy. Employees may install software only from the Software Center. Any other application requires an IT ticket with a business justification; approval takes up to 3 business days. Browser extensions that access company data must be reviewed by the Security team.

Email and Phishing Guidelines. Never share your password by email; IT will never ask for it. Use the 'Report Phish' button in Outlook to report suspicious messages. Emails from outside the company are marked with an [EXTERNAL] tag in the subject line.

Office Printing. Printers on every floor require badge login. Color printing is limited to 200 pages per month per employee. Confidential documents must be printed with the 'secure print' option and collected immediately.

Meeting Rooms. Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar app and can be reserved for up to 2 hours. If nobody checks in within 10 minutes, the booking is released automatically. Large rooms on the 5th floor require facilities approval for external events.
